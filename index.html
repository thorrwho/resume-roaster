Questions, yaar

Is this real AI?

Yes, when it can be: a Claude model writes the roast. If the AI is down or over its limit, a rule-based roaster in your browser takes over, and every result says which one wrote it.

Where does my resume go?

Your name, email, phone, links and ID numbers are masked in your browser first. The masked text goes to an AI service only to write the roast. This site does not store it, and the provider handles it under its own data policy. Open "See exactly what gets sent" to check.

Will the roast be mean?

It roasts the document, never you: no jokes about your name, looks or background. Pick Bestie for the gentlest version.

Why is one line real advice?

Because a roast with no takeaway is just noise. The real advice always comes with a concrete fix.
